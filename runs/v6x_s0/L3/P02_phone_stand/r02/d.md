**Concept**: A lightweight, skeletal "hook-and-brace" stand designed as a single continuous solid. It utilizes a thin base plate and a slimmed-down support spine to provide stability while strictly adhering to the phone's geometry without intersection through explicit boolean subtraction.

**Main Features**:
- **Base Plate**: A flat plate centered at $x=0, y=0$ with thickness 3mm, lying on the plane $z=0$. Footprint is constrained to $x \in [-50, 50]$ and $y \in [-50, 50]$ to ensure it stays within the 100x100mm limit (R2).
- **Support Spine**: A slimmed-down structural member (thickness $\approx$ 4mm) that follows the phone's tilt. To prevent interference (R3), the spine is generated by taking a primitive volume and performing a Boolean subtraction with the phone's volume $V_{phone}$ to ensure zero intersection.
- **Seat**: A dedicated volume integrated into the single solid, occupying $a \in [-4, 0], b \in [0, 9], |y| \le 15$ to cradle the bottom edge (R5). This feature must be intersected with the complement of the phone volume ($V_{phone}^c$) to guarantee zero intersection.
- **Lip**: A front protrusion integrated into the single solid, occupying $a \in [0, 8], b \in [9, 13], |y| \le 15$ to prevent forward sliding (R5). This feature must also be intersected with the complement of the phone volume ($V_{phone}^c$).
- **Rear Brace**: A vertical/angled support located behind the phone at $a \in [80, 95]$ and $b \in [-2, 0]$, near the mid-section $|y| \le 15$ (R4). The reduced $a$-range ensures the total $x$-span remains within the 100mm limit (R2).

**Load Path**: The weight of the phone is transferred from the seat and lip into the support spine. The spine directs this load through the rear brace and down to the base plate, which distributes the force across the desk surface ($z=0$).

**Requirement Fulfillment**:
- **R1 (Single Solid)**: All components (Base, Spine, Seat, Lip, Brace) are joined via a single boolean union operation.
- **R2 (Dimensions)**: Base is on $z=0$. Footprint is within $[-50, 50]$ for both $x$ and $y$. Max height is kept below 120mm. The Rear Brace $a$-range $[80, 95]$ prevents $x$-axis overflow.
- **R3 (No Interference)**: All features (Spine, Seat, Lip) are explicitly defined to be intersected with the complement of the phone volume ($V_{phone}^c$) to guarantee zero intersection.
- **R4 (Back Support)**: Material placed at $a \in [80, 95], b \in [-2, 0], |y| \le 15$.
- **R5 (Seat & Lip)**: Seat defined at $a \in [-4, 0], b \in [0, 9]$; Lip defined at $a \in [0, 8], b \in [9, 13]$. Both within $|y| \le 15$.
- **R6 (Stability)**: The base is centered at $x=0$ with a width of 90mm ($y \in [-45, 45]$). This wide footprint ensures the combined center of mass projects into the contact area with a $>5\text{mm}$ margin.
- **R7 (Mass)**: To prevent overshooting the 60g limit, the spine and base are designed with a maximum thickness of 3–5mm. Total target volume is $< 48,000 \text{ mm}^3$ ($\approx 59\text{g}$), providing a safety buffer below the $60\text{g}$ limit.
