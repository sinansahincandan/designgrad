**Concept**: A tripod-style wedge mount consisting of a wide rectangular base for stability and an inclined upright pillar that holds the sensor board at a 30° angle. The design uses a single boolean union workflow where the upright pillar is a solid volume that physically intersects with the base volume to ensure geometric validity and prevent `Null TopoDS_Shape` errors.

**Main Features**:
- **Base**: A rectangular footprint of $45 \times 40$ mm with a thickness of $10$ mm, positioned such that its bottom surface is at $z = 0$ to satisfy the desk contact requirement.
- **Upright Pillar**: A solid block that intersects the base, tilted such that it supports the mounting face.
- **Mounting Face**: A flat rectangular surface with dimensions $40 \times 30$ mm (ensuring an area of $1200 \text{ mm}^2 \ge 900 \text{ mm}^2$). The face is generated by rotating a vertical plane $30^\circ$ around the X-axis so that its outward normal vector is exactly $(0, \cos(30^\circ), \sin(30^\circ))$.
- **Mounting Face Position**: Located at a center height of $z = 42.5$ mm.
- **Screw Holes (Base)**: Two vertical holes (diameter 4.5 mm) located at $(\pm 20, -10)$ on the base.
- **Sensor Holes**: Two 3.2 mm diameter holes drilled along an axis parallel to the mounting face normal $(0, \cos(30^\circ), \sin(30^\circ))$, ensuring they are perfectly perpendicular to the tilted face and located $\pm 10$ mm from the face center along the X-axis.

**Load Path**: The weight of the sensor is transferred through the mounting face into the upright pillar, which distributes the force down to the wide base, where it is transferred to the desk via the contact area at $z=0$.

**Requirement Fulfillment**:
- R1: Created using a single boolean union/subtraction workflow on intersecting solid volumes.
- R2: Base holes (4.5 mm) placed at $(\pm 20, -10)$ with base bottom at $z = 0$.
- R3: Mounting face area $\ge 900 \text{ mm}^2$ with outward normal $(0, \cos(30^\circ), \sin(30^\circ))$.
- R4: Face center height is set to $42.5$ mm (within the $35\text{--}50$ mm range).
- R5: Sensor holes (3.2 mm) are perpendicular to the face, oriented along the face normal.
- R6: Base width ($45$ mm) and depth ($40$ mm) ensure that even with the 50 g sensor mass (offset 15 mm forward), the combined CoM stays within the footprint margin of $\ge 5$ mm.
- R7: Total volume is approx $2300 \text{ mm}^3$, mass $\approx 2.8 \text{ g}$ (well under $35\text{g}$).
